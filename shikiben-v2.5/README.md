## ⚡ Core Concept & Mathematics

### 1. Superposed Lagrangian of Existence

$$L_{\text{total}}(\mathbf{x}, \dot{\mathbf{x}}, t) = \Big( L_{\text{self}}(\mathbf{x}, \dot{\mathbf{x}}) + \gamma_{\text{d}} \cdot L_{\text{taido}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}_{\text{safe}}(t)) \Big) + \lambda(\text{Virtue}) \cdot L_{\text{ego}}(\mathbf{x})$$

* **$L_{\text{self}}$ (Reality):** Natural motion as it is and the ground state of relationships.
* **$L_{\text{ego}}$ (Appearance / Attachment):** Distortion potential generated by local fitting and bias.
* **$\lambda(\text{Virtue})$ (Virtue Factor):** Scaling that undergoes a phase transition when distortion increases, automatically severing the adverse effects of $L_{\text{ego}}$.

### 2. Unified Equation of Motion for the Four Virtues

$$\dot{\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{P}_{\text{rei}}(\mathbf{x}) \Big[ \mathbf{f}_{\text{gi}}(\mathbf{x}) + \gamma_{\text{d}} \, \mathbf{g}_{\text{Taidou}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}_{\text{safe}}(t)) - \lambda(\text{Virtue}) \cdot \nabla_{\mathbf{x}} L_{\text{ego}}(\mathbf{x}) \Big] + \mathbf{S}_{\text{jin}}(\mathbf{x})$$

* **Propriety ($\mathbf{P}_{\text{rei}}$):** An orthogonal projection operator that blocks intrusion into dangerous boundaries and converts motion into tangential sliding.
* **Righteousness ($\mathbf{f}_{\text{gi}}$):** Objective driving force that drives the system toward the target point.
* **Benevolence ($\mathbf{S}_{\text{jin}}$):** Multi-body interaction vector achieving both exclusion and entrainment (resonance) with others.
* **The Great Way ($\mathbf{g}_{\text{Taidou}}$ & $\mathbf{x}_{\text{safe}}$):** The safe baseline axis and restoring gradient that absorb distortion to self-update.
