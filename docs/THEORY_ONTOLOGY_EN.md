# "Shikiben" Summary: Mathematical & Ontological Analysis

This document provides a formal algebraic and semiotic reduction of the foundational epistemology, ontology, and societal structures (Law, Culture, Civilization, Religion) established in the treatise **"Shikiben" (識扁)**. This mathematical expansion forms the philosophical bedrock for the dynamical operators 
```math
(\mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{rei}}, \mathbf{S}_{\mathrm{jin}}, \lambda(E))
```
implemented in the Shikiben Dynamics control framework.

## 1. Structural Decomposition of Existence

Existence (the total state space) is hierarchically decomposed as a superposition of **Authentic Reality** (perceptible matter/nature) and **Ego-Distortion / Phenomenon-Representation (象 - Sho)**.

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{Existence} &= \text{Authentic Reality} + \text{Ego-Distortion (Sho)} \\
&= \text{Perceptible Entities} + \text{Conceived / Recalled Representations} \\
&= \text{Authentic Reality} + (\text{Phenomena} + \text{Representations}) \\
&= \text{Authentic Reality} + \left\{ (\text{Mundane Phenomena} + \text{Extraordinary Phenomena} + \text{Unknown Phenomena}) \right. \\
&\quad \left. + (\text{Representations of Mundane} + \text{Representations of Extraordinary} + \text{Unknown Representations}) \right\} \\
&= \text{Authentic Reality} + \left\{ (\text{Profane/Ke} + \text{Sacred/Hare} + \text{The Other}) + (\text{Intellect/Chi} + \text{Cognition/Shiki} + \text{The Other}) \right\} \\
&= \text{Authentic Reality} + \left\{ (\text{Self} + \text{The Other}) + (\text{Ego} + \text{The Other}) \right\}
\end{aligned}
```

### Ontological Baseline Definitions
* **Home (故郷)**: The Authentic Reality that forms the precondition of the Self.
* **World (世界)**: The Representation that forms the precondition of the Ego.

\begin{aligned}

## 3. Algebraic Reduction of Social Constructs

### 3.1 Law (法) — Algebraicization of Rei (礼)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Law} &= (\text{Emotion seeking to mend tears with those sharing the same Home})(\text{Image} + \text{Story} + \text{Text}) \\
&= (\text{Rei})(\text{Image} + \text{Story} + \text{Text})
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3.2 Culture (文化) — Algebraicization of Gi (義)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Culture} &= (\text{Emotion caring for those similar to the Self})(\text{Image} + \text{Song} + \text{Story} + \text{Text}) \\
&= (\text{Gi})(\text{Image} + \text{Song} + \text{Story} + \text{Text})
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3.3 Civilization (文明) — Algebraicization of Jin (仁)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Civilization} &= (\text{Emotion caring for all human beings})(\text{Text}) \\
&= (\text{Jin})(\text{Text}) \\
&= (\text{Jin})(\text{Pictographic Text} + \text{Phonetic Text}) \\
&= \text{Pictographic Civilization} + \text{Phonetic Civilization} \\
&= (\text{World discerning signs predicting all phenomena}) + (\text{World viewing existence as constituted by Logos}) \\
&= (\text{World grounded in the Tao}) + (\text{World grounded in Idea})
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3.4 Religion (宗教) — Algebraicization of Awe & Reverence (畏敬)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Religion} &= (\text{Despair over the defeat of Civilization})(\text{Image} + \text{Name} + \text{Song} + \text{Story} + \text{Text}) \\
&\to (\text{Emotion fearing the extraordinary and revering the return to the mundane})(\text{Image} + \text{Name} + \text{Song} + \text{Story} + \text{Text}) \\
&= (\text{Awe})(\text{Pictographic Lineage}) + (\text{Awe})(\text{Phonetic Lineage}) \\
&= (\text{Religion grounded in the Tao}) + (\text{Religion grounded in Idea}) \\
&= \text{Pantheism} + \text{Monotheism}
\end{aligned}
$$
